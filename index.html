About

I achieved a first class honors undergraduate degree in International Social and Political Studies from University College London specializing in Political Theory and spent a successful year abroad at the University of Tokyo studying the Kyoto School of Philosophy and Heidegger.

My particular domains of interest are philosophy and film especially Heidegger, Nietzsche, Schmitt, Hobbes, and Rousseau in the former case and Tarkovsky, Tarr, and Malick in the latter.

I also have varying degrees of familiarity with Spanish, French, and Japanese.
